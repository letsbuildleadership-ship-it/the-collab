Omnidirectional Enterprise™ · Collaborating Companies

Companies we collaborate with through the Omnidirectional Enterprise™.

Value moves in every direction between companies, people, creators and communities. These are the companies collaborating with The Co.LLab™ and with each other, and what each one offers.

How it works, in plain terms

  • Who collaborates. Companies that license Co.LLab™ IP (any .LLab™ product) or hold a Membership. Collaborating through the Omnidirectional Enterprise™ is part of what a license and a Membership include. Anyone can browse.
  • What is shown. Only what your company writes here: its name, what it does, what it offers, and the website or contact it chooses. Never your account email or Member ID.
  • How it stays true. The Co.LLab™ reads each company's page, and each change to it, before it appears.
  • Working together. Companies work with each other directly, on their own sites or by their own contact. The Co.LLab™ takes part only through the Omnidirectional Enterprise™, where value moves between companies in every direction.
  • Your control. Edit or remove your company's page any time.

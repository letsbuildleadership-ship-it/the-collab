The Co.LLab Founders Organization™ · Community Economic Development

The problem

Unequal access to entrepreneurial education, enterprise infrastructure, capital networks, and professional relationships can constrain small-enterprise formation, local investment, economic mobility, and regional development.

The solution

The Co.LLab Founders Organization™ is part of Omnidirectional Enterprise™. It builds benefactor partnerships: Founders and investors who fuel the continued building of The Co.LLab™ and its Leadership Infrastructure™, are permanently recognized, and help sustain the communities it serves. It is not a nonprofit today; as The Co.LLab™ grows, it invests back out through scholarships, angel investment and charitable donations.

What this organization is for

One organization. A different purpose.

The Co.LLab™ has three distinct parts. The Co.LLab Founders Organization™ is where investors back the building of The Co.LLab™ itself. It is not a membership, an enterprise engagement or a product.

★

The Co.LLab Founders Organization™

Benefactor partnerships. Community, sustained.

Today, an investment stream: Founders fund the continued building of The Co.LLab™’s infrastructure and are permanently recognized. Next, it invests back out into people, companies and nonprofits.

B

Partnership™

For enterprises.

A paid engagement for companies that want The Co.LLab™ to build Leadership Infrastructure™ with them. Every Founder can put what they paid toward a Partnership™, as a one-time credit.

P

The .LLab™ products

For people and teams.

Licensed tools, guides and IP you buy and keep to do the work yourself. Founding is not a purchase of these products and is not a Membership.

Today · Benefactor partnerships

Back the build. Be recognized. Sustain community.

Inside Omnidirectional Enterprise™, Founders are benefactor partners. You fuel the continued building of The Co.LLab™ and its Leadership Infrastructure™ early, you are recognized for it permanently, and the value you help create moves back out to sustain the community around it.

01

What you fund

The continued building of The Co.LLab™.

Founding investment goes toward building the infrastructure of The Co.LLab™: its Leadership Infrastructure™, its IP and the Omnidirectional Enterprise™ ecosystem around it.

02

How you are recognized

Permanently, with your own Founder Number.

Every Founder receives a permanent Founder Number, a digital Founder Card and a place on the Legacy Wall, plus 20% off eligible future products.

03

What you build

A legacy, not a transaction.

A founding investment builds the legacy of The Co.LLab™ and moves value across Omnidirectional Enterprise™. It carries no ownership stake or financial return.

Why founding early matters

The ones who build first are remembered first.

Founding sits inside Omnidirectional Enterprise™, where Communities and Capital meet, and it follows The IV Phases™ of Leadership Infrastructure™ like everything The Co.LLab™ builds. Right now, The Co.LLab™ is being built. Founders are the people who make that possible, and the earliest of them shape it most.

01

Your number is your place

Founder Numbers are issued in order as Founders join, and yours is permanent. The earliest numbers belong to those who backed the build first.

02

You fund the build itself

Early founding investment goes into the infrastructure while it is being built, when each contribution moves the work furthest.

03

Recognized from day one

Every Founder is named on the Legacy Wall on this page and carries a digital Founder Card from the moment they join.

04

Benefits that start now

20% off eligible future products from day one, everything you paid credited toward a Partnership™, and The .LLab™ products free to keep at every level.

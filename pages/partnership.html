The problem — Without partnerships, an enterprise carries it alone.

The problem

Without partnerships, an enterprise carries it alone.

Traditional enterprise partnerships often operate through fragmented networks, unequal bargaining power, and disconnected resource allocation, limiting the integration of intellectual property, products, capital, and organizational capabilities needed to generate shared economic value and competitive advantage.

The solution

Practice Omnidirectional Enterprise™.

The Co.LLab™ Partnership Program provides enterprises with a structured opportunity to practice Omnidirectional Enterprise™ by connecting proprietary intellectual property, products, leadership infrastructure, and cross-sector capabilities to unlock commercial opportunities, strengthen market positioning, and develop new pathways for innovation, investment, and enterprise growth.

Partners purchase a license to access and apply Co.LLab IP™ — not ownership of it. Results depend on how each enterprise applies it; no investment, revenue, or exclusivity is promised.

Clear Terms — Clear terms. Lasting value.

Clear Terms

Clear terms. Lasting value.

Here's exactly how it works.

  • One payment. Each partnership is a one-time purchase at the tier price shown. No subscription and no automatic charges.
  • All sales are final. All sales are final once access is unlocked, subject to applicable law.
  • What you keep. Licensed products stay in your account for your internal enterprise use after your term. The documents you create with them, like your own vision, playbook, and succession plans, belong to your enterprise.
  • Memberships. The .LLab Membership™, the .LLab Library Card™ (with The .LLab Library™), and The .LLab Journal™: Partner 1 year, Strategic 2 years, Enterprise 3 years, then renew yearly at regular prices. Omnidirectional memberships are lifetime.
  • New releases. Every tier keeps each new Co.LLab™ release made while its membership is active. Custom development and bespoke services are excluded unless stated in writing.
  • Partner-only IP. Each tier adds one partner-only product: The .LLab Legacy Foundations Framework™, The .LLab Legacy Infrastructure Plan™, The .LLab Legacy Operating Systems Manual™, and The .LLab Legacy Preservation Vault™. Each tier includes the products of the tiers below it.
  • Seats. Partner 1, Strategic 3, Enterprise 5, Omnidirectional 10 (including the purchaser). One seat = one person's account, added by single-use invite link. No shared accounts.
  • Self-guided. Every tier is licensed IP your team puts to work on its own. Consulting, implementation, and custom work are not included and require a separate written agreement.
  • IP ownership and license. All Co.LLab™ IP is owned by Ashley E. Clasby and licensed through The Co.LLab™. Every tier is a license under the Co.LLab™ IP license: internal use only. No training, consulting, client use, co-branding, redistribution, sublicensing, or resale without written authorization.

  • Connected to The Co.LLab Founders Organization™. The Co.LLab Founders Organization™ is a separate offering for founders. It is not a partnership tier, and no Founders product is included in any partnership. Every Founder can apply the full amount they paid for founding as a one-time credit toward a Partnership, applied automatically at checkout while signed in.

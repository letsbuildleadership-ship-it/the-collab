If you disappeared for 30 days,

Most businesses are built on one person at the top. When that person steps away, or the market shifts, it starts to dry up. The Co.LLab™ builds the infrastructure that makes your business hold.

The problem

Your business depends on you. When you step away or the market shifts, it dries up.

The vision lives in one head. Nothing is written down. The team waits on one person for every decision and burns out. That's hierarchy: everything leans on whoever sits at the top.

The solution

Build it into the company. So it holds without you.

A Partnership licenses Co.LLab™ IP to your business. Your team uses it to write down your vision, your playbook and your succession, and run on them. Every partner gets:

  • The IV Phases™: the infrastructure, licensed to your business
  • The Library: a book club for leaders, so your team thinks and moves together
  • The .LLab Journal™: a magazine of culture, intelligence, and edge

Omnidirectional Enterprise™

Your partnership doesn't stop with you.

It connects your company to an ecosystem with no hierarchy, where value moves between businesses, nonprofits, communities, people, technology, capital, knowledge + IP, and creators. When one direction dries up, value keeps moving through the others.

Tap any circle to explore that part of the ecosystem.

Our mission: to build leadership infrastructure that empowers people and organizations to transform human potential into performance, sustainable enterprise value, and lasting legacy.

Clear Terms

Clear terms. Lasting value.

Here's exactly how it works.

Read the full terms
  • One payment. Each partnership is a one-time purchase at the tier price shown. No subscription and no automatic charges.
  • All sales are final. All sales are final once access is unlocked, subject to applicable law.
  • What you keep. Licensed products stay in your account for your internal business use after your term. The documents you create with them, like your own vision, playbook, and succession plans, belong to your company.
  • Memberships. .LLab Membership™, the .LLab Library Card™ (with The Legacy Library™), and The .LLab Journal™ are included for 12 months from purchase, then renew yearly at regular prices. Omnidirectional memberships are lifetime and never renew.
  • New releases. Enterprise keeps new Co.LLab™ releases from its first 12 months; Omnidirectional keeps new releases from its first 3 years. Partner and Strategic don't include new releases. Custom development and bespoke services are excluded unless stated in writing.
  • Seats. Partner 1, Strategic 3, Enterprise 10, Omnidirectional 25 (including the purchaser). One seat = one person's account, added by single-use invite link. No shared accounts.
  • Self-guided. Every tier is licensed IP your team puts to work on its own. Consulting, implementation, and custom work are not included and require a separate written agreement.
  • IP ownership and license. All Co.LLab™ IP is owned by Ashley E. Clasby and licensed through The Co.LLab™. Every tier is a license under the Co.LLab™ IP license: internal use only. No training, consulting, client use, co-branding, redistribution, sublicensing, or resale without written authorization.
  • Separate from the Founders Organization. The Founders Organization is a separate offering for founders. It is not a partnership tier, and no Founders product is included in any partnership.
  • An investment in your business. The Co.LLab™ provides the IP, the infrastructure, and the ecosystem. What it becomes is up to you, because you know how to run your business better than anyone. Results depend on how each business applies it. A Partnership may qualify as a business tax deduction; speak with your tax strategist.

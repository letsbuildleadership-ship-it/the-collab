Built with our members

What changed.

Every improvement to The Co.LLab™ IP and the Member Portal™, and where it came from: the IP review, or member feedback. Feedback is never quoted and members are never named. Members can send feedback at the end of any quest.

2026-11-25 · IP review · All guided IP

The IV Phases™ in every guided IP

Every guided quest now carries all four phases (Foundations, Infrastructure Planning & Management, Internal Operating Systems and Preservation), with new prompts written for each IP, clearer questions where a prompt was only a label, and a four-phase build guide: the deliverable of each phase and how to put it to work. Long quests offer a short path as well as the full path.

2026-11-25 · IP review · Member Portal™

Phase Readiness, your Legacy Statement and commitment check-ins

Your portal now totals every finished quest into one Phase Readiness view, drafts one Legacy Statement from your own answers (yours to edit, private, and downloadable as a PDF), and brings each quest commitment back at 30, 90 and 365 days so you can record what happened.

2026-11-25 · IP review · Every quest

Team mode and the Team Quest Facilitator Guide

Several leaders can now work the same quest, each in their own account, and see where their answers differ. Answers are shared with the team only after a person accepts, and stop being shared when they leave. A one-page facilitator guide shows how to run a quest with a team.

2026-11-25 · IP review · The .LLab Leadership Infrastructure Diagnostic™

Nonprofit and Small Enterprise editions of the Diagnostic

The same Diagnostic in the language of a nonprofit (board, staff, volunteers, donors) and of a small enterprise (an owner, a small team, cash flow). Owning the Diagnostic unlocks both editions.

2026-11-25 · IP review · The .LLab Succession Quest™

A free Succession quest

A free quest for every signed-in member: what must stay true when leadership changes, who carries each role, how the handover is practiced, and where the succession record lives.

2026-11-25 · IP review · The .LLab Collaborating Enterprises

The .LLab Collaborating Enterprises: introductions, offers and more ways to find each other

Filter by all nine directions of the Omnidirectional Enterprise™. Request an introduction to an enterprise that welcomes them (their email stays private). Listings can carry a member offer, join the Nonprofit & Community category with skill and volunteer requests, and flag that they are seeking a successor or an apprentice.

Goal and Measures

What we are building toward, and exactly how we count it.

← The models

From goal to outcome

The goalEnterprises operating as one connected system
The outcomeRevenue generated and sustained across the whole network

The goal and the outcome

Leadership Infrastructure™, lived first hand.

The goal

A network that moves as one

To show, first hand, what Leadership Infrastructure™ really means: enterprises that stop operating alone and start operating as one connected system.

The outcome

Revenue that keeps flowing

To push economic enterprise forward: generating and sustaining revenue across the whole network, with value that holds across geopolitical lines instead of depending on any one of them.

How we measure it.

Qualitative

What changes

  • Enterprises collaborate as peers, across sectors.
  • Leadership is documented and passed on, not lost with one person.
  • Communities are sustained where enterprises operate.
  • Ethics are held as part of the infrastructure.
Quantitative

What we count

  • Enterprises in the network.
  • Sectors and regions connected.
  • Revenue flowing between enterprises in the network.
  • Share of returns reinvested.
  • Enterprises completing each of The IV Phases™, through Preservation.

Measured as the network grows, not promised in advance.

Counted phase by phase

The IV Phases™, start to finish.

One of the things we count is how many enterprises complete each phase, all the way through Preservation.

  2. Phase IIPlan it.Infrastructure Planning & Management
  3. Phase IIILive it.Internal Operating Systems
  4. Phase IVPass it on.Preservation

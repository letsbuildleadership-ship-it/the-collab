Glossary

The Co.LLab™ terms, defined.

IP (Intellectual Property) · Co.LLab IP™

Infrastructure planning IP, licensed to keep. The Co.LLab IP™ Intellectual Property License & Terms of Use applies to every Co.LLab™ product, membership, and partnership.

Omnidirectional Enterprise™

An economic enterprise system that connects people, organizations, products, intellectual property, capital, and resources in one ecosystem. It is the operating model of The Co.LLab™.

The IV Phases™

Foundations, Planning, Operating, and Preservation: a common way to decide and organize.

Leadership Infrastructure™

The frameworks, systems, community, and research of The Co.LLab™ that support community-based enterprise development and local economic participation.

Infrastructure Analysis™

A review of the intellectual property you license: what to strengthen and build next. Included with .LLab Membership™.

Partnership™

A structured opportunity for businesses to practice Omnidirectional Enterprise™ through licensed IP, products, leadership infrastructure, and cross-sector capabilities.

The .LLab™

Research, theory, applied practices, and proprietary intellectual property developed into structured knowledge resources that support innovation, strategic decision-making, and enterprise development.

The .LLab Products™

Every product from The Co.LLab™ on one page: memberships, The IV Phases™, .LLab Creators™, partnerships and free tools.

.LLab Membership™

Every Phase I–IV .LLab™ product, unlocked while you are a member, plus the Infrastructure Analysis™ and ongoing access to the frameworks, systems, community, and research of Leadership Infrastructure™.

.LLab Library Card™

Founding access to the digital library and curated resource stream inside The Co.LLab™ ecosystem.

The .LLab Journal™

The intellectual-property and thought-leadership layer of The Co.LLab™ ecosystem. Magazine-style analysis connecting business, economics, geopolitics, technology, culture, and society.

.LLab R.A.T.™ · Research And Theory

Analysis of business, economic, political, and geopolitical systems to identify structural shifts, evaluate strategic risks, assess market exposure, and interpret emerging commercial opportunities.

.LLab Creators™

Creative enterprise infrastructure that strengthens intellectual property ownership, commercialization, revenue diversification, and creators’ ability to capture and retain economic value within the creative economy.

The Co.LLab Founders Organization™

Being developed as a community-based nonprofit initiative intended to pursue 501(c)(3) status, with the aim of expanding access to entrepreneurial education, business resources, and leadership infrastructure.

.LLab Legacy Library™

The books and scholarly publications of The Co.LLab™ founder, separate from the .LLab Library Card™.

.LLab Atelier™

A design-led product portfolio for living well, spanning home design, home-office design, workplace and office design, community spaces, furniture, home goods, fashion and related products. In development.

.LLab Impact™

Strategic planning, communication, governance, and resource allocation to support community development, institutional coordination, sustainability, and measurable social and economic performance.

.LLab Legacy Ventures™ · Technology

Technology development, intellectual property strategy, capital pathways, and enterprise development that support commercialization and the long-term economic value of innovative assets.

.LLab Transport™

Operating infrastructure for the businesses that move goods and people by road, air and water: trucking, planes and boats. In development.

Co.LLab+™ Agriculture

Infrastructure for farmers, cattle producers and the food products industry, feeding people and animals. In development.

Co.LLab+™

Intended to be the e-commerce side of The Co.LLab™, where members list their website, highlight a product or service, and advertise. In development.

.LLab Collaborating Companies

Companies we collaborate with through the Omnidirectional Enterprise™.

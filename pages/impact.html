Impact™

Building leadership, building good.

Select Founders Organization products are designated Impact Products™ — a fixed 10% of the purchase price supports a partner nonprofit, tracked openly and reconciled by hand.

How Impact Products work

01

A product is designated

The Co.LLab™ marks a specific product as an Impact Product and sets a fixed percentage — currently 10%.

02

The allocation is recorded

At the moment of purchase, that percentage of the sale is logged to an internal ledger, tied to the nonprofit partner.

03

Totals are public

Aggregate totals per partner are shown below — no purchase details, no customer information, just the running total.

04

Funds are remitted

The Co.LLab™ reconciles and sends the allocated amount to each partner directly, on a regular cadence.

Honesty matters here: Stripe is not automatically splitting or transferring these funds to the nonprofit partners in real time. Unless a partner has its own Stripe Connect account on file, the 10% obligation is tracked internally and remitted by The Co.LLab™ directly to the organization. The ledger and totals below reflect what has been allocated and what has been sent.

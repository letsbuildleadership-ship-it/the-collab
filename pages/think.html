R.A.T.™ — Research And Theory

Some ideas begin as: “Wait… what if?”

R.A.T.™ is the research and theory side of The Co.LLab™ ecosystem — where unusual questions, observations, experiments, cultural patterns, and ideas can be explored. Curious. Scholarly. Weird. Provocative. Intellectually playful.

The trilogy

Research → Theory → Infrastructure.

R.A.T.™ is the research and theory laboratory. The larger Co.LLab™ ecosystem turns ideas into usable infrastructure — an intellectual ecosystem, not simply a consulting company's methodology.

A Co.LLab concept

Metacognitive Infrastructure Thinking™

Thinking about the infrastructure behind thinking itself. Instead of asking “how do we solve this problem,” ask: “what structure is producing the way we're approaching this problem?” Then: “what infrastructure would allow people to think, decide, create, lead, and operate differently?”

Bridging theory and technology

.LLab Intelligence Infrastructure™

Intelligent systems should understand human behavior rather than simply demand human compliance. This is a proprietary approach to designing intelligent systems around how people actually behave — one of the bridges between The Co.LLab™'s theoretical work and future technology.

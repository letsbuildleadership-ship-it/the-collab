Team quests · one page

Run a quest with your team.

A facilitator guide for running a Co.LLab™ quest in one meeting, written for The .LLab Foundation Strength Snapshot™ (free) and usable with any quest. It keeps the structure and the language the same, so every team works the IP the same way.

Before · 15 minutes

Set it up

  1. Sign in, open the quest and choose Start a team on this quest. Invite each leader by email.
  2. Tell people what is shared: once they accept, their answers to this quest are shown to this team only, and they can leave at any time.
  3. Ask everyone to answer alone, before the meeting. For the Snapshot that is four scores (Clarity, Consistency, Support, Direction) and one commitment. No discussing scores in advance.

In the room · 60 minutes

Run the meeting

  1. Purpose (5 min). Read the quest's opening line aloud. Foundations come first: everything else in The IV Phases™ leans on them.
  2. Where we differ (10 min). Open Compare our answers. Read the differences in silence. A gap of two points or more on a score is worth a conversation.
  3. The gaps, one at a time (25 min). Start with the widest gap. Ask each person: what did you see that made you score it that way? Listen for evidence, not opinions.
  4. Through The IV Phases™ (10 min). Use the build guide on the quest page: what each phase should leave you with, and how to put it to work.
  5. One commitment (10 min). Agree on one action with one owner and a date. Each leader records it as their quest commitment.

The facilitator

Hold the structure

  • Stay neutral: you guide the process, not the answers.
  • Start with the lowest-scoring dimension, not the favorite one.
  • Keep the words of the IP: Foundations, Infrastructure Planning & Management, Internal Operating Systems, Preservation.
  • Answers are shared with the team only. Nothing leaves the room unless the person who wrote it agrees.

After

Keep it alive

  • Each leader finishes the quest in their Member Portal™; the report is saved there.
  • The commitment comes back at 30, 90 and 365 days. Bring the check-ins to the next team meeting.
  • Ready to go further? The quest's next step builds infrastructure on what you found.

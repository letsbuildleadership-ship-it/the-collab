Engagement termsClear from the start

How engagements work.

  • Applicants are not employees of The Co.LLab™, and submitting an application does not create employment.
  • Independent Sales Partner engagements are intended to be independent contractor arrangements where legally permissible.
  • Worker classification is determined in compliance with applicable federal, state and international laws. No particular classification, including 1099 status, is automatic or promised.
  • International engagements must comply with the applicable laws of the jurisdiction in which the work is performed.
  • Every commission arrangement requires a separate written agreement.
  • No employment, compensation or engagement is guaranteed by submitting an application.

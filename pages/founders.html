A distinct offering

Omnidirectional Enterprise™ — value built through collaboration.

The Founders Organization is separate from The .LLab. It is how companies partner with The Co.LLab™: their participation provides the capital and support to build Leadership Infrastructure™, and that value moves back through the Omnidirectional Enterprise™ ecosystem — connecting companies, creators, organizations, nonprofits, communities, intellectual property, opportunity, and legacy.

The Four Phases of Leadership Infrastructure™

01

Foundations

Founder participation lays the foundation — the capital and support that let The Co.LLab™ build Leadership Infrastructure™ around human potential.

02

Infrastructure Planning & Management

That support is directed into the infrastructure the Omnidirectional Enterprise™ needs — and how companies, creators, organizations, and communities connect to it.

03

Internal Operating Systems

The systems, tools, and frameworks built from that support serve the whole ecosystem, moving value in multiple directions.

04

Preservation

Value returns through the Omnidirectional Enterprise™ — to companies, creators, nonprofits, communities, intellectual property, opportunity, and lasting legacy.

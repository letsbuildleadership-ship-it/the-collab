The Co.LLab Founders Organization™ · Community Economic Development

The problem

Unequal access to entrepreneurial education, business infrastructure, capital networks, and professional relationships can constrain small-business formation, local investment, economic mobility, and regional development.

The solution

The Co.LLab Founders Organization™ is part of Omnidirectional Enterprise™. It builds benefactor partnerships: Founders and investors who fuel the continued building of The Co.LLab™ and its Leadership Infrastructure™, are permanently recognized, and help sustain the communities it serves. It is not a nonprofit today; as The Co.LLab™ grows, it invests back out through scholarships, angel investment and charitable donations.

What this organization is for

One organization. A different purpose.

The Co.LLab™ has three distinct parts. The Co.LLab Founders Organization™ is where investors back the building of The Co.LLab™ itself. It is not a membership, a business engagement or a product.

Next · The investment strategy

Built to invest back out.

As The Co.LLab™ grows, The Co.LLab Founders Organization™ is built to invest in three places: in people, in companies and in other nonprofits. Each pillar has its own job. Together they are designed to feed one another, so every dollar Founders put in keeps building Leadership Infrastructure™ in the community.

01

Scholarships

Invest in people. Build the pipeline.

The strategy starts with people. Scholarships are intended to open the work of The Co.LLab™ to people who would not otherwise have access to it, so talent is not limited by who can pay.

Who it is for
Aspiring and early-stage founders, emerging leaders and the people who will run community organizations.
What it funds
Entrepreneurial education, leadership development and access to The IV Phases™ of Leadership Infrastructure™, from Foundations onward.
Why it is strategic
Scholarship recipients are the future founders, operators and board members of the companies and nonprofits the other two pillars support.
Omnidirectional Enterprise™
People · Knowledge + IP · Phase I, Foundations
02

Angel investment

Invest in companies. Grow local enterprise.

Ideas need capital to become companies. The angel investment pillar is intended to provide early-stage, mission-aligned investment in community-based companies that are building with Leadership Infrastructure™.

Who it is for
Community-based companies at the early stage, including those started by scholarship recipients and Founders.
What it funds
Early capital to start and grow, paired with the structure to plan, operate and last: Infrastructure Planning & Management and Internal Operating Systems.
Why it is strategic
Investment is intended to be patient and mission-first. Any return is intended to be reinvested in the mission, renewing the funds for the next scholarship, investment and donation. Founders themselves take no ownership stake or financial return.
Omnidirectional Enterprise™
Capital · Businesses · Phases II and III
03

Charitable donations

Invest in nonprofits. Strengthen communities.

Some needs are met best by organizations already doing the work. Charitable donations are intended to back other nonprofits, as Founders already do today through Blazing Smiles and Wavecraft Oceans Project.

Who it is for
Nonprofits serving communities, chosen for clear purpose and the ability to sustain their work.
What it funds
Grants and donations toward programs, with the option to build the organization’s own Leadership Infrastructure™ so its impact lasts beyond any one leader.
Why it is strategic
Strong nonprofits make strong communities, and strong communities produce the people and companies the first two pillars invest in.
Omnidirectional Enterprise™
Nonprofits · Communities · Phase IV, Preservation

How the three work together

  1. FoundersFounders back the organization through a founding option.
  2. ScholarshipsPeople gain the education and Leadership Infrastructure™ to build.
  3. Angel investmentThe companies they start receive early, mission-aligned capital.
  4. Charitable donationsNonprofits are strengthened to serve the community.
  5. ReinvestmentReturns and growth flow back into the next round of the strategy.

Guiding principles

  • Mission first: every investment, scholarship and donation must build Leadership Infrastructure™ in a community.
  • Reinvest, never distribute: what the strategy earns goes back into the mission, not to Founders.
  • Connected, not merged: Founders, Partnership™ and The .LLab™ products each keep their own role and lead into one another through Omnidirectional Enterprise™.
  • Built in phases: today, Founders fund the building of The Co.LLab™; each pillar opens as The Co.LLab™ grows.

This strategy describes intentions, not commitments; amounts, criteria and timing will be set as each pillar opens.

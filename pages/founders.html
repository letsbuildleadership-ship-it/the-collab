Build at Any Amount

Build the infrastructure of Omnidirectional Enterprise™ at the level that fits you. Every founding payment, from $50, makes you a Founder, and your running total sets your founding level.

Secure checkout by Stripe. At checkout, add the name you want on the Legacy Wall: yours or your company’s. Every founding payment also counts toward your Partnership™ credit, and 10% supports Blazing Smiles or Wavecraft Oceans Project.

Every founding level includes a permanent Founder Number, a digital Founder Card, a place on the Legacy Wall, 20% off eligible future products from The Co.LLab™ while signed in, and everything you paid credited once toward a Partnership™, applied automatically at checkout while signed in. Every level adds free .LLab™ products from its phase, delivered to your library at checkout, and 10% of every founding payment supports Blazing Smiles or Wavecraft Oceans Project.

Let’s Build Your Legacy Through Omnidirectional Enterprise™. Founding is a standalone BUILD offering, not an investment and not a membership: it carries no ownership stake, profit share or financial return, and it is not a tax-deductible charitable gift. See the legal and tax notes below.

Legal & tax notes

What founding is, and what it is not.

  • A standalone BUILD offering. Founding funds the continued building of The Co.LLab™ and its Leadership Infrastructure™, in exchange for the recognition and products listed for your level. It is not a Membership, not a tier of The .LLab™ and not a Partnership™; it connects to them only through the free products and the one-time Partnership™ credit described above.
  • Not an investment or a security. Founding carries no equity, ownership, profit share, interest, dividend, voting right or financial return of any kind, and nothing on this site is an offer to sell securities. The “Investment Strategy” describes how the organization intends to invest outward in the future, not a return to Founders.
  • Not a tax-deductible charitable gift. The Co.LLab Founders Organization™ is not a nonprofit or tax-exempt organization today, so founding payments are not charitable contributions for tax purposes. The share that supports Blazing Smiles or Wavecraft Oceans Project is passed on by The Co.LLab™ and does not make your payment a deductible gift.
  • A future nonprofit is a plan, not a promise. Any future nonprofit formation, tax-exempt status, programs, amounts and timing are intentions and are not guaranteed.
  • Purchases and licenses. Checkout is handled securely by Stripe. Products granted with a founding level are licensed under the IP License & Terms of Use, and purchases follow the Clear Terms.
  • Not professional advice. Information on this site is general and is not legal, tax, accounting or investment advice. Whether any payment is deductible as a business expense depends on your circumstances; ask your own attorney or tax professional.

Open to everyone · Any amount · Everyone who builds becomes a Founder

Build at Any Amount

Build the infrastructure of Omnidirectional Enterprise™ at the level that fits you. Every founding payment, from $50, makes you a Founder, and your running total sets your founding level.

Secure checkout by Stripe. At checkout, add the name you want on the Legacy Wall: yours or your enterprise’s. Every founding payment also counts toward your Partnership™ credit, and 10% supports Blazing Smiles or Wavecraft Oceans Project.

Every founding level includes a permanent Founder Number, a digital Founder Card, a place on the Legacy Wall, 20% off eligible future products from The Co.LLab™ while signed in, and everything you paid credited once toward a Partnership™, applied automatically at checkout while signed in. Every level adds free .LLab™ products from its phase, delivered to your library at checkout, and 10% of every founding payment supports Blazing Smiles or Wavecraft Oceans Project.

Let’s Build Your Legacy Through Omnidirectional Enterprise™. Founding is a standalone BUILD offering, not an investment and not a membership: it carries no ownership stake, profit share or financial return. Founding is a business purchase: every level includes licensed .LLab™ IP products, so it may be deductible as a business expense; consult your tax professional.

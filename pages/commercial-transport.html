.LLab Transport™.

Trucking, planes and boats. Infrastructure for the businesses that keep goods and people moving. In development.

Technology · .LLab Legacy Ventures™ → Explore The .LLab™ →

The problem

Commercial transport runs on equipment, routes, regulations and people. When ownership, operating knowledge and succession live in a few heads, the operation is fragile.

The solution

.LLab Transport™ applies The IV Phases™ to transport operators: define the business, plan routes and capacity, run it on written systems, and preserve it for the next owner.

.LLab Transport™ · What it covers

Built to move, built to last.

In development

01

Trucking

Freight, fleets and the people who drive them.

Owner-operators and fleets, run on written systems.

In development

02

Planes

Aviation operators and the crews and systems behind them.

Operations that can be documented, handed on and audited.

In development

03

Boats

Vessels, marine operators and ports.

Waterborne transport run as a business, not a person.

In development

04

Operating infrastructure

The IV Phases™ for transport: define, plan, live and pass on the operation.

One foundation across road, air and water.

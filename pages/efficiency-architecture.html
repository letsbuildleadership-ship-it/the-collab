The .LLab Efficiency Architecture™ · Spaces as Infrastructure

The problem

Fragmented design and product markets can make it difficult for people and organizations to access thoughtfully developed goods that support the environments, identities, and practical demands of enterprise.

The solution

The .LLab Efficiency Architecture™ develops spaces designed as infrastructure: ergonomic efficiency homes, office studio spaces, art studio spaces and community spaces, connecting design, intellectual property, and commercial enterprise.

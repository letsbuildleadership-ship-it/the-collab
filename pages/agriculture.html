Co.LLab+™ Agriculture™.

Farmers, cattle and the food products industry, for people and animals. In development.

Technology · .LLab Legacy Ventures™ → Explore The .LLab™ →

The problem

Farms, ranches and food producers carry land, livestock, seasons and families. When the operation, its records and its succession are not written down, what took generations to build is hard to pass on.

The solution

Co.LLab+™ Agriculture applies The IV Phases™ to agriculture and food: define the operation, plan the land, herd and supply, run it on written systems, and preserve it for the next generation.

Co.LLab+™ Agriculture · What it covers

From the land to the table, and to the next generation.

In development

Farming

Crops, land and the seasons that run them.

Farm operations written down so they can be handed on.

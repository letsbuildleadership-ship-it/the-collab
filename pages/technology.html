Technology · The .LLab Legacy Ventures™ · Technology Commercialization & Enterprise Development

The problem

Gaps between technological innovation, intellectual property ownership, capital allocation, regulatory strategy, and commercialization can prevent emerging technologies from achieving market adoption, attracting investment, and generating durable enterprise value.

The solution

Technology · The .LLab Legacy Ventures™ connects technology development, intellectual property strategy, capital pathways, and enterprise development to support commercialization, market competitiveness, and the long-term economic value of innovative assets.

The .LLab AV Productions™

All things sound and video, for the occasions that bring people together and the people who create them.

Audio and visual production for every kind of gathering, from concerts and auctions to weddings, reunions and memorials, and the infrastructure behind the musicians and creators who need the rest built around the craft. In development.

  • Sound: everything audio, from system design to live mixing and recording
  • Video: everything visual, from videography to live visuals and production
  • Music applications and DJ partnerships
  • Concerts, auctions, and community and sponsored events, with sound, MC and auctioneer support
  • Private and milestone events: weddings, reunions, celebrations and memorials
  • Infrastructure for musicians and creators who are exceptional at the craft and need the production built around it
  • Event operations: staging, safety, permits, admission and crowd flow planned in
  • Home office integrations: connected workspaces where sound, video and communication work together, so you can lead and create from home

Every event is built on The IV Phases™: purpose, ownership, operations and continuity.

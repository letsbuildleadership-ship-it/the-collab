How the IP works in The .LLab™

The IP is the method. The .LLab™ is where you work it.

Each product is a proprietary methodology and tool. Membership is the environment that lets you access, apply and progress through them together, across The IV Phases™.

On its own

Buy any product to keep: the licensed fillable PDF, its guided quest in your Member Portal and the Infrastructure Analysis™ of your own answers to it.

Together, inside The .LLab™

Every finished quest adds to one private record: Phase Readiness across The IV Phases™, your next step in each phase, your Legacy Statement, 30, 90 and 365-day check-ins, comparisons with your earlier runs, and the mission, vision, purpose and values you already wrote offered back in the next IP that asks for them.

Included or separate

Included with The .LLab Membership™: every IV Phases™ product (14, Phases I to IV), plus the Infrastructure Analysis™. Separate purchases: The .LLab Creators™ line and The .LLab Blueprint™ magazine. Partner-only IP: included with Partnership™ levels. Free tools: open to everyone.

# The Co.LLab™ — project brief (read this instead of re-auditing)
Static site + Netlify Functions. Live: https://wearethellab.com (Netlify publishes `main`, no build step). Strategy: `STRATEGY.md`. State: `PROJECT_CHECKPOINT.md`.

## Commands
`npm test` (198 tests) · `npm run photos` (bulk photo intake) · `npm run gallery` (Lookbook page parked; `--force` to rebuild) · `node scripts/build-ads.mjs` · `node scripts/build-partnership-page.mjs` (tier tables from pricing) · `node scripts/build-ip-license.mjs` · `node scripts/build-hub.mjs` (all-products hub from pricing) · `node scripts/generate-pdfs.mjs <key>` (product PDFs) · `npm run pitch` (rebuilds assets/pitch/ pitch + deck from pricing.json; the "Rebuild pitch" workflow does it automatically on main, a test fails if stale) · `node scripts/build-nav.mjs` (header + footer menus on every page) · `node scripts/apply-oe-metatron.mjs` (draws every Omnidirectional Enterprise™ model on the site as Metatron's Cube from `scripts/oe-metatron.mjs`: 13 equal circles, 78 lines, inner ring independent, outer ring external; keeps each page's links and lit nodes; idempotent. The pitch and deck draw the same figure from `scripts/pitch/metatron.mjs`) · `node scripts/oe-figures.mjs` (redraws the four Omnidirectional Enterprise Model™ figure images, assets/img/oe/ + scripts/pitch/assets/, from the same model; the interactive versions on omnidirectional-enterprise.html are drawn by `js/oe-figures.js`) · `node scripts/build-quests.mjs` (free-tool quests) · `node scripts/build-paid-quests.mjs` (39 paid IP quests: 25 read from the workbooks, 14 hand-written for guides, manuals and magazines; needs pdftotext) · `node scripts/block-paid-files.mjs` (run after adding any paid PDF: blocks it from public download). New product with no Stripe link yet: add it to pricing.json with `stripe_pending: true` (hub shows "Available soon"); run the owner-only `admin-setup-stripe-products` function (curl with X-Admin-Token; creates the Stripe product, price and Payment Link, `?dry=1` to preview), save its `results` as links.json and run `node scripts/apply-stripe-links.mjs links.json` (writes the ids, clears the flags, rebuilds the hub and pitch)

## Members & owner tools
Owner pages are private: `/admin/*` is served by `netlify/functions/admin-page.js` only to a signed-in owner session (token -> signed cookie) and looks like a missing page to everyone else; the owner signs in at the private entry address `/admin/<ADMIN_ENTRY>/<page>` (ADMIN_ENTRY lives in Netlify env, never in code). Never add static admin files outside that route.
Free vs paid: the free-tool PDFs are open to everyone (no account). A free account works the quests on the free tools (and open quests) and gets the Infrastructure Snapshot™ (`previewFor`) that prompts Membership; paid IP, its quests, the Infrastructure Analysis™ (full analysis, outcomes, PDF report) is Membership (or owning that product). Portal quests (`content/quests`, `netlify/functions/quest.js`) · .LLab Collaborating Companies (`pages/collaborating-companies.html`, part of a license/Membership, owner-approved) · owner pages `admin/customers.html`, `admin/ip-feedback.html` (IP review: feedback + ideas + weekly email, nothing left undecided) · `admin/ip-analysis.html` (review all IP, run the analysis, decide on each recommendation), `admin/collaborating-companies.html`.

## Page pattern (luxury / shopping-site style)
Header → **model ribbon** (the only element repeated site-wide, plus the Home/Think/Partnership model) → hero → the page's own main points + products → **Continue** strip (2 links). **Each product/idea lives on exactly one page; other pages link, never repeat.** Opening pages stay short: hook → products to buy → a Learn more row of buttons to subpages for the long copy (e.g. `founders.html` → `founders-purpose.html`, `founders-strategy.html`). Home = hero + the economic case of Omnidirectional Enterprise™ (no contents list; the footer Navigate + More lists are the site index); long copy lives on each page. `pages/all-products.html` is the product hub (every product, one line, link only; generated). Removed copy is archived in `docs/archive/removed-blocks.html` (404 on the live site). No sticky bottom button on any page.

## Rules
- **Every marketing tactic (ad, page, offer, email, funnel step) is modeled under Omnidirectional Enterprise (node) × The IV Phases (1–4).** Tag new ads in `scripts/build-ads.mjs` (`TAG`); a test enforces it. See `STRATEGY.md` §2.
- Approved copy and prices are fixed: no invented claims, testimonials, scarcity or earnings. Prices/Stripe links only via `content/pricing.json`.
- Founders, .LLab, Partnerships stay distinct. Founders ≠ membership. Founders and Partnership link to each other (Omnidirectional flow): every Founder gets what they paid for founding as a one-time Partnership credit (`founderPartnershipCredit`, applied by checkout-create, marked used in fulfill); Founder/Legacy Founder also get a free .LLab product (`grants`); no Founders product is in any Partnership. One Omnidirectional Legacy Wall (`netlify/functions/legacy-wall.js`, on `pages/omnidirectional-enterprise.html#legacy-wall`, with the #blueprint map of where The Co.LLab uses each area): everyone who takes part, by OE area; names only, never removed; other pages link to it.
- Nav and footer menus come from one source: `scripts/build-nav.mjs` (run it after adding/renaming a page; tests enforce that the top menu reaches every page with exact page titles). Top menu (inline dropdowns at >=1380px, Menu-button accordion below): Home · The Co.LLab™ ▾ (The enterprise: About, The Co.LLab™ Glossary; The Co.LLab+ Marketplace, The Co.LLab+ Agriculture) · Omnidirectional Enterprise™ ▾ (The Models, The Enterprise System Model, Goal and Measures, Research) · The .LLab™ ▾ (3-column panel; the Journal Free Preview lives on The .LLab Journal page, not the menu: ecosystem & membership; research, theory & Intelligence Infrastructure™; Creator Infrastructure™ & ventures) · Partnership™ ▾ (business navigation to pull customers and partners: Partnership™; For companies: Why Partner With Us, Partnership Levels & Pricing, What Your Company Gets, Partner-Only IP; Partner with confidence: Clear Terms, Our Partners, Talk to Us About Partnership) · Founders Organization™ ▾ (Founders, Purpose, Investment Strategy) · Get in touch ▾ (Contact Us, Meet the Founder) · Language (🌐, Google Translate, trademark names kept) · Sign in. Footer = secondary links only: Explore · Members · Contact · Legal (IP License, Clear Terms) · Follow (Instagram). Founders is a standalone BUILD offering: never call founding an investment, a gift/donation or a membership. Every .LLab and Co.LLab+ name takes "The" in front. Never say "company/companies" in site copy: always "enterprise/enterprises" (URLs and code names like `collaborating-companies`, `companyName` stay). Structure: The Co.LLab™ = umbrella enterprise and model enterprise (Omnidirectional Enterprise™ on The IV Phases™); The Co.LLab+™ = community preservation and sustainability systems, and The Co.LLab+ Marketplace™ (community.html, in development: every participating enterprise lists and sells there; built to drive each .LLab sector); The .LLab™ = commerce (each .LLab page a future company); The Co.LLab Founders Organization™ = future nonprofit. The Omnidirectional ribbon stays beneath the header.
- Text must stay AA-contrast; smallest text is bold white; text on photos sits on a frosted panel (`.lookbook`, `.tile`, `hero--lookbook`).
- New photos: `npm run photos` (JPGs in git-ignored `photos/inbox/`; blur third-party logos with `add-photo.mjs --blur`).

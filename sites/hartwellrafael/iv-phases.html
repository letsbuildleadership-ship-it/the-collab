The IV Phases™ of Leadership Infrastructure™

One model, four phases.

Hartwell & Rafael Enterprise is structured on The IV Phases™. Every engagement — a single project or an ongoing relationship — moves through the same four phases, from Foundations to Preservation.

  1. I

    Phase I · Define it.

    Foundations

    Purpose, capabilities, organizational direction, and opportunity.

    Every engagement starts by defining what the enterprise is for, what it can do and where the opportunity lies — for a client, a project or a professional.

    Services in this phase: Branding · Marketing · Product Design

  2. II

    Phase II · Plan it.

    Infrastructure Planning & Management

    Talent sourcing, project planning, resource allocation, and coordination.

    The right people and resources are planned and put in place: who does the work, when, with what, and how it is coordinated.

    Services in this phase: Talent Sourcing · Project Coordination

  3. III

    Phase III · Live it.

    Internal Operating Systems

    Relationships, workflows, communication, execution, and delivery.

    The work runs: relationships, workflows and communication carry the project through execution to delivery.

    Services in this phase: Graphic Design · Web Development · Photography · Content

  4. IV

    Phase IV · Pass it on.

    Preservation

    Intellectual property, institutional knowledge, relationships, sustainable enterprise value, and legacy.

    What the work created is kept: intellectual property, knowledge and relationships become sustainable enterprise value and legacy.

    In every engagement: the work, knowledge and relationships created are carried into Preservation.

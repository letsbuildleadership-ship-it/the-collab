About

One enterprise. Every direction.

Hartwell & Rafael Enterprise is an Omnidirectional Enterprise connecting businesses, organizations, and independent creative professionals through creative services, specialized expertise, project coordination, and commercial opportunity.

Who we are

The coordinating enterprise.

We bring together the people who need creative and specialized work done and the professionals who do it best, then coordinate the work from first conversation to delivery.

Businesses and organizations come with a goal: a brand to build, a product to launch, a campaign to run, a team to assemble. Independent creative professionals come with capability: design, development, photography, content, strategy. Hartwell & Rafael Enterprise sits at the centre as the coordinating enterprise, so expertise, resources and opportunity reach the place they create the most value.

Hartwell & Rafael Enterprise is built on the Omnidirectional Enterprise Model™ and runs every engagement through The IV Phases™ of Leadership Infrastructure™.

Mission & Vision

Why we exist, and where we are going.

Mission

To connect human capabilities with commercial opportunity through integrated creative services, strategic coordination, and collaborative enterprise infrastructure — creating sustainable value for businesses and professionals.

Vision

To advance an interconnected creative economy where expertise, intelligence, resources, and opportunity move across traditional organizational boundaries, enabling collaboration, sustainable enterprise growth, and lasting legacy.

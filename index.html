Omnidirectional Enterprise™ · The economic case

Generating value and capturing value are different processes.

Economic performance depends not only on creating value, but on the systems governing how resources are coordinated, intellectual property is owned, decisions are made, innovations reach markets, and returns are captured and reinvested. Omnidirectional Enterprise™ is an economic enterprise system that connects people, organizations, products, intellectual property, capital, and resources in one ecosystem.
